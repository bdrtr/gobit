# Two readings of "province" — measured 2026-09-08

Evidence for [ADR 0067](../adr/0067-a-province-is-the-sub-country-unit-and-not-the-district.md).

## The two readings

**The schema's.** `internal/modules/tax/migrations/000001_tax_init.up.sql` says
in its header: *"tax_region is a tax region: a country root, or a province under
that root"*, and names the class — *"a sub-country unit (a US state, a Canadian
province, a TR province)"*. Two constraints stand behind it: the parent/root
uniqueness, and the rule that *"a province row's country CANNOT DIFFER from its
root's"*, written to stop "a DE province under the TR root" coming into being
silently.

**The shipping test's.** `internal/e2e/shipping_test.go` filed
`City: "Istanbul", Province: "Kadikoy"` and asserted the round trip with the
reason *"a domestic carrier prices on the district"*. Kadikoy is an ilce, under
the il of Istanbul.

## Why they never met

`internal/workflows/cart/tax.go` documents its own request field: *"ProvinceCode
is the state/province code and is ALWAYS EMPTY in this phase: the cart only holds
a region, the address is not visible from this surface."*

So the value the shipping test writes never reaches the schema that defines the
word. Both suites were green on both readings, and would have stayed green.

## The silence, counted

Thirty-two declarations of `Province` or `ProvinceCode` exist in production Go.
Twenty-one are generated by sqlc. Of the eleven written by hand, **eleven had no
comment** — cart (six), inventory (two), order (two) and the checkout plan (one).

Two more were already spoken for by a group comment naming the field, which is
how this repository writes an address: *"Address1, Address2, City, Province and
PostalCode are location detail"*. The gate honours that idiom; it was written to
after the first run reported those two as bare.

## The book cannot carry one

`customer_address` has NO sub-country column of any kind — no province, no
state, no district. A shopper picking a saved address therefore cannot bring a
province to the cart, because the cart's copy is made by the caller and the book
never held one.

That is not closed here. The tax request sends the field empty, so a column added
now would be one nothing reads.

## What the gate holds

`TestEveryProvinceFieldSaysWhatItMeans` refuses a hand-written declaration with
no doc comment. Mutation-proved both ways:

- Removing the comment from the cart's model field fails it, naming the file and
  the line.
- Changing the field names it looks for to a word the tree does not use fails the
  blindness floor: *"no province field was found at all; the scan has gone
  BLIND."*

It cannot read the comment. What it removes is the step the second reading
actually took.
